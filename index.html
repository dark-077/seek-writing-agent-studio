Before & After Review

修改前后对比与课堂展示

Evidence-based revision

Before Revision

The person who inspires me most is my high school English teacher, Ms. Lin. She was always patient and responsible. She often encouraged us when we felt nervous before exams. I still remember that she stayed in the classroom after school to help some students with their English. She also told us that learning English was not only for tests, but also for seeing a bigger world. Because of her, I became more confident in English learning. I think she is a good teacher and I respect her very much.

After Revision

The person who has influenced me most is my high school English teacher, Ms. Lin. At that time, I was afraid of speaking English in public because I often worried about making mistakes. One evening before the final exam, she noticed that several of us were still confused about the speaking task. Instead of simply giving us answers, she stayed in the classroom for nearly an hour, listened to each student patiently, and helped us improve our expressions sentence by sentence. From her, I learned that a responsible teacher does not just teach a subject; she helps students discover confidence in themselves.

ReasonIt shows responsibility more clearly
